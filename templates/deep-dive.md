## The 60-second version
Say it out loud with a timer. One sentence each:
- **Context:** the product, the team, your role.
- **Problem, with a number:** what was hurting, and how much.
- **What you built:** the shape of the solution in one breath.
- **Your hardest decision:** the trade-off, and what you gave up.
- **Result, with a number:** what changed, and how you measured it.
